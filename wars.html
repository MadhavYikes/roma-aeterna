Wars of Rome

Rome's history was shaped as much by internal conflict as by conquest.

Punic Wars

Rome and Carthage fought three wars for Mediterranean supremacy.

Gallic Wars

Caesar's campaigns brought much of Gaul under Roman control.

Dacian Wars

Trajan's campaigns conquered Dacia after two major wars.

Civil Wars

Repeated struggles between rival commanders and imperial claimants reshaped the state.
