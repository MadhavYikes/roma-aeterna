Roman Sources

No single source tells the whole story. Roman history is reconstructed by combining literary and material evidence.

Tacitus

A major literary source for the Julio-Claudian and Flavian periods.

Suetonius

Biographical accounts of the early emperors, valuable for court and elite culture.

Cassius Dio

A long Greek-language history covering centuries of Roman imperial history.

Material Evidence

Coins, inscriptions, buildings and archaeological remains provide evidence independent of literary narratives.
