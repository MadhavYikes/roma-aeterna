DENARII · AUREI · SESTERTII · SOLIDI

Roman Coins

Coins are among the most abundant surviving sources for imperial portraiture, titles, religion and propaganda.

Denarius

A major silver denomination of the Republic and early Empire.

Aureus

The principal gold denomination of the imperial period before late-antique reforms.

Sestertius

A large bronze denomination widely used in the imperial monetary system.

Solidus

A late Roman gold coin introduced under Constantine and influential for centuries.
