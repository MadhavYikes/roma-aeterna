THE ARMY OF ROME

Legions & Warfare

I

The Legion

A legion was a major Roman military formation, normally commanded by a legate and divided into cohorts, centuries and smaller tactical units.

II

The Legionary

Equipment varied across centuries, but the shield, gladius, javelins and protective armour became defining features of imperial infantry.

III

The Frontier

Roads, forts, walls and permanent bases allowed Rome to move troops and maintain long frontiers from Britain to the Euphrates.

IV

Command

Imperial armies were instruments of both external conquest and internal politics; commanders who controlled troops could become emperors.
