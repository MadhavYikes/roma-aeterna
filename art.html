Roman Art

Roman art survives across public monuments, private houses, tombs, temples and imperial commissions.

Portrait Sculpture

Emperors and citizens were represented in carefully coded styles of age, authority and idealization.

Frescoes

Pompeian wall paintings preserve mythological, architectural and everyday imagery.

Mosaics

Floors and walls carried geometric patterns, mythological scenes and images of nature.

Relief

Narrative reliefs such as Trajan's Column turned imperial history into monumental visual storytelling.
