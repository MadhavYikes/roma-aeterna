193 AD

Year of the Five Emperors

The assassination of Commodus opened a succession crisis involving Pertinax, Didius Julianus, Pescennius Niger, Clodius Albinus and Septimius Severus.

Pertinax

Emperor for roughly three months.

Didius Julianus

Proclaimed emperor after the Praetorian auction.

Septimius Severus

Winner of the civil war and founder of the Severan dynasty.
