GODS · RITUAL · IMPERIAL CULT

Roman Religion

Roman religion was a public system of ritual, priesthoods and relationships with gods, ancestors and the state.

The Gods

Jupiter, Juno, Minerva, Mars, Venus and Apollo were among the best-known Roman deities.

Imperial Cult

Emperors and members of the imperial household received cultic honors in cities across the empire.

Temples

Temples, altars and sacred precincts structured civic and religious space.

Christianity

Christian communities grew inside the empire and eventually became central to imperial religious policy.
